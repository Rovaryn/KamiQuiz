In Chainsaw Man, devils are everywhere, and they do not all look alike. Some look like monsters, others like insufferable teenage girls, others still like perfectly ordinary humans. Tatsuki Fujimoto built a fairly precise set of rules around them, which the series reveals chapter by chapter. Here is the essentials to find your way around. This article avoids the big reveals at the end of the first part.

## Devils born from fear

Every devil is born from a name, an object or a concept, and is named after what it represents: the Tomato Devil, the Bat Devil, the Fox Devil. Its power depends on one thing: **how much humans fear what it embodies**. A devil linked to a trivial fear stays weak. A devil linked to a universal fear becomes formidable.

That is what makes the Gun Devil so terrifying in the series: its past attack spread immense fear around the whole world, and that fear feeds it in turn. The more humans talk about it and dread it, the stronger it gets.

Devils also have a peculiar life cycle. A devil killed on Earth is reborn in Hell, and a devil killed in Hell is reborn on Earth. The **Primal Fears**, like the Darkness Devil, embody fears so ancient that they have never been killed. They are on a completely different level from ordinary devils.

## Fiends

A devil can take possession of a human corpse. It then becomes a **fiend**. The devil keeps its personality, but it is partly limited by the body it occupies. A fiend can often be recognized by a physical detail that betrays its true nature, like Power's horns.

Power, the Blood Fiend, and Beam, the Shark Fiend, are the two best-known examples. Public Safety uses some fiends as hunters, as long as they stay under control.

## Hybrids

**Hybrids** are different. They are living humans who can turn into a devil while remaining themselves. Denji is the main example: after merging with Pochita, the Chainsaw Devil becomes his heart, and all he has to do is pull the starter cord sticking out of his chest to transform.

Other hybrids appear over the course of the series, like Katana Man and his sword arm, or Reze, the Bomb hybrid. Hybrids are much tougher than humans and regenerate by drinking blood, which makes them fearsome opponents.

## Contracts

Human hunters have no power of their own. To fight, they make **contracts** with devils. The devil lends its strength, and the hunter pays a price, often a very heavy one. Aki Hayakawa is the perfect illustration. The Fox Devil appears to devour his targets. The Curse Devil lets him kill almost any enemy, but costs him years of his life each time he uses it. The Future Devil, living in his eye, shows him a few seconds of the future.

The more powerful the devil, the higher the price. It is one of the darkest ideas in the series: devil hunters literally wear themselves out in the service of Public Safety.

## Chainsaw Man, a devil apart

Pochita is not a devil like the others. In Hell, devils fear the one they call the Hero of Hell, and for good reason: whatever Chainsaw Man devours ceases to exist, to the point of vanishing from human memory. This power, revealed gradually, explains why so many characters are interested in Denji's heart.

## The classic quiz traps

Power is a fiend, while Denji is a hybrid: the difference is at the heart of several questions. The Future Devil and the Curse Devil are two different contracts of Aki's. A devil killed on Earth is reborn in Hell, not on Earth. Finally, a devil's power depends on the fear it inspires, not on its age or its size.

With these rules in mind, the questions about devils in the Chainsaw Man quiz become much more logical.
