In Harry Potter, the first evening at Hogwarts decides seven years of a student's life. The Sorting Hat places each new student in one of the four houses, which become their family, their dormitory and their team all at once. The houses are named after the four witches and wizards who founded the school around a thousand years ago. Here is what you need to know about each of them.

## One school, four founders

Godric Gryffindor, Salazar Slytherin, Rowena Ravenclaw and Helga Hufflepuff founded Hogwarts together, far from Muggle eyes. Each wanted to admit students according to a different quality: courage for Gryffindor, ambition and pure blood for Slytherin, intelligence for Ravenclaw, and everyone else, without distinction, for Hufflepuff.

It was this disagreement, especially over admitting children born to Muggle parents, that led Slytherin to leave. Before leaving the school, he is said to have built a secret room, the Chamber of Secrets, whose opening is at the heart of the second book.

So that the Sorting would not depend on them alone, the founders handed the task to a hat that had belonged to Gryffindor and gave it their judgment. The Sorting Hat takes the student's wishes into account: Harry, who begs not to go to Slytherin, is sent to Gryffindor.

## Gryffindor

**Values:** courage, daring, chivalry. **Emblem:** a lion. **Colors:** scarlet and gold. **Head of House:** Minerva McGonagall. **Ghost:** Nearly Headless Nick.

The common room is at the top of a tower, behind the portrait of the Fat Lady, who demands a password. It is the house of Harry, Ron and Hermione, but also of Albus Dumbledore and the whole Weasley family. Its downside is well known: a taste for risk that sometimes borders on recklessness. The Sword of Gryffindor, which appears when a true member of the house needs it, plays a decisive role in the saga.

## Slytherin

**Values:** ambition, cunning, determination. **Emblem:** a serpent. **Colors:** green and silver. **Head of House:** Severus Snape, then Horace Slughorn. **Ghost:** the Bloody Baron.

The common room is in the dungeons, under the lake, which gives it a greenish light. The house has a bad reputation, because most dark wizards, Tom Riddle among them, came from it. The books nevertheless nuance that image: Severus Snape, Regulus Black and Horace Slughorn show that ambition does not mean cruelty. In the epilogue, Harry tells his son Albus Severus that he is named after a former Hogwarts headmaster from Slytherin, probably the bravest man he ever knew.

## Ravenclaw

**Values:** intelligence, learning, wit. **Emblem:** an eagle (bronze in the books). **Colors:** blue and bronze. **Head of House:** Filius Flitwick. **Ghost:** the Grey Lady.

The common room is in a tower, and you do not get in with a password: the door knocker asks a riddle, and you must answer it correctly. Luna Lovegood and Cho Chang belong to this house. It is linked to a Horcrux in the seventh book, the lost diadem of Rowena Ravenclaw, whose story is known to the Grey Lady, the founder's daughter.

## Hufflepuff

**Values:** loyalty, patience, hard work, fairness. **Emblem:** a badger. **Colors:** yellow and black. **Head of House:** Pomona Sprout. **Ghost:** the Fat Friar.

The common room is near the kitchens, behind a stack of barrels: you have to tap the right barrel in the right rhythm to open it. Often mocked, it is the house of Cedric Diggory, the Hogwarts champion in the Triwizard Tournament, and of Nymphadora Tonks. It is also the house that has seen the fewest of its students turn to the Dark Arts. Helga Hufflepuff's cup, turned into a Horcrux, is hidden in the Lestranges' vault at Gringotts.

## The House Cup

Throughout the year, students win and lose points for their house according to their results and their behavior. The house with the most points wins the cup at the end of the year. The first book ends on a famous twist: Dumbledore awards last-minute points that put Gryffindor ahead of Slytherin.

Quidditch follows the same logic. Each house has its team, and the Quidditch Cup is the other big prize of the year. Harry becomes Gryffindor's Seeker in his very first year, something that had not happened in a century.

## The classic quiz traps

Questions about the houses often trip people up on the same details. Ravenclaw's eagle is bronze in the books, while the films popularized a silver version. Slytherin's ghost is the Bloody Baron, not the Fat Friar, who belongs to Hufflepuff. The Head of Slytherin changes in the sixth book, when Snape becomes the Defence Against the Dark Arts teacher and Slughorn takes over Potions. Finally, Peter Pettigrew was indeed in Gryffindor, like the other Marauders.

These details make the difference in the Harry Potter quiz, where the category on the houses is one of the most played.
