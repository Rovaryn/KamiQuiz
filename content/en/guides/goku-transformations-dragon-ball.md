Transformations are the signature of Dragon Ball. Each new level of power reached by Son Goku has defined an arc of the series, and their order is an endless source of trick questions. Here is the complete path, from the hero's childhood all the way to Dragon Ball Super.

## The Great Ape (Oozaru)

First of all, Goku is a Saiyan, even though he does not know it for a long time. As a child, he has a tail, and the sight of the full moon turns him into a giant ape, uncontrollable and colossally strong. It is in this form that he unknowingly killed his adoptive grandfather, Son Gohan. His tail is cut off several times, and the moon itself ends up destroyed by Master Roshi during the 21st World Martial Arts Tournament… before Piccolo blows it up again at the start of Dragon Ball Z.

## Kaio-ken

During the Saiyan saga, Goku trains with King Kai and learns the Kaio-ken. It is not a transformation in the strict sense, but a technique that temporarily multiplies his power at the cost of great physical strain. He uses it against Vegeta, times two, then times three and four. The Kaio-ken comes back much later, combined with the Super Saiyan Blue form.

## Super Saiyan

This is the most famous transformation in the manga. On Namek, Krillin's death before Goku's eyes triggers such rage that he becomes a Super Saiyan: spiky golden hair, turquoise eyes, a yellow aura. The fight against Frieza that follows is one of the best-known in manga history.

During the Android saga, Goku and Gohan learn to stay transformed all the time to reduce its energy cost, a form fans call Full Power Super Saiyan.

## Super Saiyan 2

The first to reach this level is not Goku but his son Gohan, during the fight against Cell. The hair stands up even more and lightning surrounds the aura. Goku later shows this form during the Buu saga, notably against Vegeta under Babidi's control.

## Super Saiyan 3

Still during the Buu saga, Goku reveals a brand-new form: golden hair falling to the small of his back and no eyebrows. Very powerful, it also wears its user out very quickly, especially since Goku has come back from the afterlife for a single day. Goten and Trunks also reach this level in their fused form, Gotenks.

## Super Saiyan God

In Dragon Ball Super and the film Battle of Gods, Goku faces Beerus, the God of Destruction. Through a ritual in which five pure-hearted Saiyans pass their energy to him, he becomes a Super Saiyan God: red hair and a slimmer build. It is the first divine transformation of the series.

## Super Saiyan Blue

Super Saiyan Blue combines divine power with the Super Saiyan form: blue hair and a blue aura. Goku and Vegeta use it for the first time against the returning Frieza. Goku later combines it with the Kaio-ken against Hit, and again in the Tournament of Power.

## Ultra Instinct

During the Tournament of Power, facing Jiren, Goku reaches Ultra Instinct, a mastery that even most gods do not have. His body reacts on its own, without going through conscious thought. He first reaches an incomplete form, with black hair and a silver aura, before reaching the mastered form, with silver hair.

## What about Super Saiyan 4?

Super Saiyan 4, with its red fur and black hair, appeared in Dragon Ball GT, an anime series that was not based on a manga by Akira Toriyama. That is why it was long kept apart from the official transformations, and why it is one of those questions where you need to read the wording carefully.

## The classic quiz traps

The first Super Saiyan 2 in history is Gohan, not Goku. The Kaio-ken is not a Saiyan form but a technique of King Kai's. Super Saiyan God requires the help of five Saiyans in addition to Goku, and Vegeta reaches it later through his own training. Finally, the silver form of Ultra Instinct only comes after a first, incomplete version.

With this order in mind, the “Transformations” category of the Dragon Ball quiz becomes much more approachable.
