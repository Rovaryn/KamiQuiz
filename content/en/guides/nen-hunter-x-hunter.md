Nen is the power system of Hunter × Hunter, and one of the richest in action manga. Yoshihiro Togashi introduces it during the Heavens Arena arc, then develops it until it becomes the heart of every fight. Understanding its rules completely changes how you read the series, and it is essential for the hard questions in the quiz.

## What is Nen?

Every living being gives off a life energy, **aura**. In most people, it leaks out of the body uncontrolled. Nen is the art of mastering this aura. There are two ways to learn it: slowly, through meditation, or brutally, by receiving a blast of aura from another user that forces the aura nodes open. Gon and Killua learn it from Wing during the Heavens Arena arc, where they discover that some fighters use a force they cannot see.

## The four basics

Every user starts with four principles:

- **Ten**: keeping your aura around your body like armor, instead of letting it leak out.
- **Zetsu**: closing your aura nodes to make it disappear, which lets you hide and recover.
- **Ren**: producing a larger amount of aura than normal.
- **Hatsu**: using your aura in a personal way, in other words developing your own technique.

Hatsu is what people usually call a character's power. And the form it takes depends on the user's category.

## The six categories

Every person has a natural affinity for one of the six categories:

- **Enhancement**: making your body or an object stronger. Gon is the textbook example, as is Uvogin of the Phantom Troupe.
- **Transmutation**: giving your aura the properties of something else. Killua turns his into electricity, Hisoka into a substance that is both elastic and sticky.
- **Emission**: detaching your aura from your body and projecting it. Leorio and Razor, the game master of Greed Island, belong here.
- **Conjuration**: creating objects out of aura. Kurapika conjures his chains.
- **Manipulation**: controlling living beings or objects. Illumi Zoldyck manipulates people with his needles.
- **Specialization**: any power that fits no other category. Chrollo, the leader of the Phantom Troupe, steals other people's powers. Kurapika becomes a Specialist when his eyes turn scarlet.

The categories are arranged in a hexagon. The closer a category is to your own, the easier it is to master. An Enhancer progresses well in Transmutation and Emission, its neighbors, but struggles with Specialization, on the opposite side. Hisoka sums up these affinities with a personal theory that links a personality to each category, such as the idea that Enhancers are simple and straightforward.

## The water divination test

To find out your category, you use water divination: you place a leaf on a glass of water and perform Ren around the glass. The result depends on the category:

- **Enhancement**: the amount of water increases.
- **Transmutation**: the taste of the water changes.
- **Emission**: the color of the water changes.
- **Conjuration**: impurities appear in the water.
- **Manipulation**: the leaf moves.
- **Specialization**: some other change happens.

Gon makes the glass overflow. Killua makes the water sweet, which confirms he is a Transmuter.

## Advanced techniques

Once the basics are learned, users combine these principles. **Gyo** concentrates aura in the eyes to see what is hidden. **In**, an advanced form of Zetsu, hides the aura of an attack. **En** spreads aura around you to sense everything within it. **Ko** concentrates all of your aura in a single point, at the cost of having no defense anywhere else. Gon's famous “Rock!” relies on this idea. **Ken** spreads reinforced aura over the whole body for defense. **Ryu** means moving aura from one place to another during a fight, a decisive skill against high-level opponents.

## Restrictions and vows

The most original rule of Nen is that of **conditions**. A user can impose limits or risks on themselves to strengthen a technique. The heavier the restriction, the greater the power. Kurapika is the most famous example: his chain that can neutralize members of the Phantom Troupe can only be used against them, and he stakes his life on keeping that rule.

The same principle explains Gon's transformation against Neferpitou during the Chimera Ant arc. By sacrificing all his future potential, he gains colossal power in the moment, with very heavy consequences.

## The classic quiz traps

Hisoka is a Transmuter, not a Manipulator. Kurapika was born a Conjurer and only becomes a Specialist when his eyes are scarlet. Zetsu hides your aura, while In hides a technique: the two are often confused. Finally, in the water divination test, the leaf moves for Manipulation, and the color changes for Emission.

Nen is the most demanding theme in the Hunter × Hunter quiz. With these landmarks, it also becomes one of the most rewarding.
