In Demon Slayer, the Hashira, or Pillars, are the nine most powerful swordsmen of the Corps. They are the ones who face the Upper Ranks, the most dangerous demons serving Muzan Kibutsuji, and they are the ones who train the next generation. Knowing who they are, and which Breathing Style each one has mastered, helps you follow the whole series. Be warned: the end of this article mentions events from the final arcs.

## The Demon Slayer Corps in a few words

The Demon Slayer Corps is not recognized by the government of the Taishō era. It is led by the Ubuyashiki family, whose head, Kagaya, is called “Oyakata-sama” by the members. Demon slayers are ranked in ten levels, from Mizunoto, the lowest, to Kinoe, the highest. Above these ranks are the Pillars, called Hashira.

A swordsman becomes a Hashira by killing one of the Twelve Kizuki or by slaying fifty demons, provided they have reached the highest rank. There are never more than nine Hashira at the same time.

Every demon slayer uses a breathing technique that greatly boosts their physical abilities, and a Nichirin Blade, forged from an ore that absorbs sunlight. The color of the blade changes depending on who wields it, and often points to the Breathing Style that suits them.

## Breathing Styles: a family tree

Every Breathing Style comes from the same source, Sun Breathing, created by Yoriichi Tsugikuni several centuries ago. His students, unable to reproduce it as it was, drew five basic styles from it: Water, Flame, Wind, Stone and Thunder. These in turn gave rise to variants, adapted to the bodies and temperaments of their users.

Love Breathing comes from Flame, Sound from Thunder, Mist from Wind, Serpent and Flower from Water, and Insect from Flower. Tanjiro starts with Water Breathing before rediscovering the Hinokami Kagura, the Dance of the Fire God passed down in his family, which is linked to Sun Breathing.

## The nine Hashira

**Giyu Tomioka, the Water Hashira.** He is the one who spares Nezuko in the first chapter and sends Tanjiro to his former master, Sakonji Urokodaki. A man of few words, he created an eleventh form of Water Breathing, Dead Calm.

**Kyojuro Rengoku, the Flame Hashira.** Enthusiastic and generous, he is at the center of the Mugen Train arc, adapted into a film in 2020. His fight against Akaza, Upper Rank Three, is one of the most memorable moments of the series.

**Tengen Uzui, the Sound Hashira.** A former shinobi, he wields two huge cleavers joined by a chain and analyzes his opponents' rhythm with his “Musical Score”. He leads the Entertainment District mission alongside Tanjiro, Zenitsu and Inosuke.

**Mitsuri Kanroji, the Love Hashira.** Gifted with extraordinary muscle strength, she uses a thin, flexible blade like a whip. She is a former apprentice of Rengoku.

**Muichiro Tokito, the Mist Hashira.** He became a Hashira only two months after holding a sword for the first time, and he is the youngest of the nine. He has lost his memory, and the Swordsmith Village arc partly gives it back to him.

**Gyomei Himejima, the Stone Hashira.** Blind and unanimously recognized as the strongest of the Hashira, he fights with an axe and a spiked flail joined by a chain. He cries almost constantly and prays a lot.

**Sanemi Shinazugawa, the Wind Hashira.** Covered in scars and openly hostile to Nezuko at first, he is the older brother of Genya, whom Tanjiro meets during the Final Selection.

**Obanai Iguro, the Serpent Hashira.** Accompanied by his white snake Kaburamaru, he has eyes of different colors and a winding fighting style. He is very close to Mitsuri.

**Shinobu Kocho, the Insect Hashira.** Too frail to behead a demon, she got around the problem by developing poisons made from wisteria, a flower demons cannot stand. Her older sister, Kanae, was the Flower Hashira.

## Hashira Training

After the Swordsmith Village arc, the Hashira organize a joint training program to prepare the demon slayers for the final battle. Each Hashira takes charge of one aspect: stamina, flexibility, speed, strength. This arc also dwells on the Demon Slayer Mark, which appeared during the fights in the Swordsmith Village: a symbol that forms on the body of certain fighters and greatly boosts their abilities, at the cost of a much shorter life expectancy.

## The classic quiz traps

Questions about the Hashira often play on lineages. Kanae Kocho was not the Insect Hashira but the Flower Hashira, and Kanao, the Kocho sisters' student, uses Flower Breathing. Sound Breathing comes from Thunder, not Wind. Gyomei Himejima is blind, which often surprises people. Finally, Tanjiro, Zenitsu and Inosuke never officially become Hashira, even though they fight at their side until the very end.

These distinctions make up a good share of the hard questions in the Demon Slayer quiz, especially in the category on Breathing Styles.
