All the comedy of Spy × Family rests on one idea: four characters live under the same roof, each with a secret that could bring the family crashing down, and almost none of them knows the others' secrets. Tatsuya Endo exploits this premise chapter after chapter. Here is who knows what, and why it all holds together anyway.

## The backdrop: a fragile peace

The story takes place in a world reminiscent of the Cold War. Two neighboring countries, **Ostania** in the east and **Westalis** in the west, live in a tense peace after a long war. The Forger family lives in Berlint, the capital of Ostania.

Westalis is worried about Donovan Desmond, leader of Ostania's National Unity Party, who could rekindle the conflict. The problem is that he almost never shows himself. His only public appearances are at the social gatherings of Eden Academy, reserved for the best students and their parents. His son Damian is enrolled there.

## Loid Forger, the spy

**What he hides:** Loid is Twilight, Westalis's best spy, working for the WISE agency. His mission, Operation Strix, is to get close to Desmond. To do that, he needs a child enrolled at Eden, and therefore a family.

**What he doesn't know:** that his daughter reads minds, that his wife is an assassin, and that his dog sees the future. Loid thinks he is the only one playing a part in the house. His official cover is a psychiatrist at Berlint General Hospital.

## Yor Forger, the assassin

**What she hides:** Yor is the Thorn Princess, an assassin who works for a secret organization called Garden. By day, she is a clerk at City Hall.

**What she doesn't know:** that her husband is a spy. Yor agrees to marry Loid for a practical reason: a single woman her age attracts the attention of the secret police. The marriage protects her as much as it protects Loid.

Yor's secret is all the more delicate because her younger brother, **Yuri Briar**, works for the State Security Service, Ostania's secret police. He hunts precisely spies like Twilight, without suspecting that his brother-in-law is one, or that his sister is an assassin.

## Anya, the telepath

**What she hides:** Anya reads minds. She is the result of a secret experiment, where she was designated Test Subject 007, before escaping and going from one orphanage to the next.

**What she knows:** everything. Anya chose Loid at the adoption precisely because she read in his mind that he was a spy, which thrills her. She also very quickly finds out that Yor is an assassin. But she cannot say anything: if her parents learned about her powers, or discovered each other's secrets, the family could break apart. So Anya spends a good part of the series protecting the others' secrets, often clumsily and always hilariously.

At Eden Academy, she also has to earn eight Stella to become an Imperial Scholar, or at least befriend Damian, for her father's mission to succeed. Eight Tonitrus bolts, on the other hand, would get her expelled.

## Bond, the dog who sees the future

**What he hides:** Bond, a big white dog, comes from a secret military program, Project Apple, which gave him the ability to see the future.

**Who knows:** only Anya, because she sees Bond's visions when she reads his mind. Together, they foil several disasters without the adults knowing. Bond joins the family after a bomb plot that his visions helped to stop.

## Why the family holds together

Each of the Forgers entered this marriage for the wrong reasons. Loid wanted a cover, Yor an alibi, Anya a family with a spy father. But over the episodes, the roles they play become sincere. That is the strength of the series: behind the spy gags, Spy × Family tells how a fake family becomes a real one.

## The classic quiz traps

Twilight is Loid's code name, and Nightfall is that of Fiona Frost, another WISE spy. Yor works for Garden, while Yuri belongs to the State Security Service: two different camps within Ostania. Anya is Test Subject 007, and Bond comes from Project Apple. Finally, it takes eight Stella to become an Imperial Scholar, and eight Tonitrus bolts to be expelled.

With these landmarks, the Spy × Family quiz becomes almost as much fun as the series.
