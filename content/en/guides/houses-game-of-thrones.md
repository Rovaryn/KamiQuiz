Westeros is a continent of families. Before the battles and the betrayals, Game of Thrones rests on a handful of great houses, each ruling a region of the Seven Kingdoms. Knowing them means understanding the alliances, the grudges and the marriages that drive the plot. Here are the main ones, with their words, their sigil and their seat.

## Stark

**Words:** “Winter is Coming”. **Sigil:** a gray direwolf on a white field. **Seat:** Winterfell, in the North.

The Starks have been the wardens of the North for thousands of years, kings for a long time before bending the knee to Aegon the Conqueror. Their words are the only ones among the great houses that do not boast: they warn of a danger to come. Eddard Stark, his wife Catelyn and their children (Robb, Sansa, Arya, Bran, Rickon) are at the center of the first season, together with Jon Snow, presented as Eddard's illegitimate son. Each child receives a direwolf pup in the first episode.

## Lannister

**Words:** “Hear Me Roar!”. **Sigil:** a golden lion on a red field. **Seat:** Casterly Rock, in the Westerlands.

The richest house in Westeros owes its fortune to its gold mines. Its official words are less famous than the saying that follows it everywhere: “A Lannister always pays his debts”. Tywin, the patriarch, and his children Cersei, Jaime and Tyrion form one of the most memorable families of the series.

## Targaryen

**Words:** “Fire and Blood”. **Sigil:** a red three-headed dragon on a black field. **Seat:** Dragonstone.

Coming from ancient Valyria, the Targaryens conquered Westeros with their dragons around three centuries ago and founded the dynasty that forged the Iron Throne. Overthrown by Robert Baratheon's rebellion fifteen years before the start of the series, they are represented by Daenerys and her brother Viserys, in exile across the Narrow Sea. The series House of the Dragon tells the story of their civil war, the Dance of the Dragons.

## Baratheon

**Words:** “Ours is the Fury”. **Sigil:** a crowned black stag on a gold field. **Seat:** Storm's End, then Dragonstone for Stannis.

Robert Baratheon is king at the start of the series, after leading the rebellion against the Targaryens. His brothers Stannis and Renly each claim the throne when he dies, which sets off the War of the Five Kings. Cersei's children officially bear the Baratheon name, but their real father is Jaime Lannister.

## Greyjoy

**Words:** “We Do Not Sow”. **Sigil:** a golden kraken on a black field. **Seat:** Pyke, on the Iron Islands.

The Ironborn live by raiding and despise those who farm the land, hence their words. Theon Greyjoy, the Starks' ward, is one of the characters whose path changes the most over the seasons, alongside his sister Yara and their uncle Euron.

## Tyrell

**Words:** “Growing Strong”. **Sigil:** a golden rose on a green field. **Seat:** Highgarden, in the Reach.

Masters of the most fertile region of Westeros, the Tyrells use their harvests and their marriages as political weapons. Margaery and her grandmother Olenna, nicknamed the Queen of Thorns, are among the most skillful players of the game of thrones.

## Martell

**Words:** “Unbowed, Unbent, Unbroken”. **Sigil:** a red sun pierced by a golden spear. **Seat:** Sunspear, in Dorne.

Dorne is the only region the Targaryens never conquered by force: it joined the Seven Kingdoms through a marriage. Oberyn Martell, the Red Viper, arrives in King's Landing in the fourth season with a score to settle with the Lannisters.

## Arryn and Tully

**Arryn:** “As High as Honor”, a white falcon and moon on a blue field, seated at the Eyrie, in the Vale. **Tully:** “Family, Duty, Honor”, a silver trout on a red and blue field, seated at Riverrun, in the Riverlands.

These two houses are linked to the Starks through the Tully sisters: Catelyn married Eddard Stark, and Lysa married Jon Arryn, the Hand of the King whose death starts the plot of the first season.

## The classic quiz traps

The official words of House Lannister are “Hear Me Roar!”, not “A Lannister always pays his debts”, which is only a saying. “Winter is Coming” is the Stark words, not just a line of dialogue. The Baratheon sigil is a stag, and the Greyjoy sigil is a kraken, two animals often mixed up in the answer choices. Finally, Dorne was never conquered by the dragons, which explains the Martells' independent spirit.

These landmarks cover most of the “Houses” category in the Game of Thrones quiz, one of the most played.
