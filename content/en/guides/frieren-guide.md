Frieren begins with an ending. The hero Himmel and his companions have defeated the Demon King, the kingdom celebrates their return, and the story could stop there. But Frieren, the party's mage, is an elf: she has already lived for more than a thousand years and will live much longer still. The manga by Kanehito Yamada and Tsukasa Abe tells what happens next, as her human friends grow old and she realizes, too late, that she never really knew them. Here are the landmarks to follow the series and ace the quiz.

## The hero's party

Four adventurers ended the Demon King's reign after a ten-year journey. **Himmel**, the human hero, generous and a little vain, is at its center. **Heiter**, the priest, is a bit too fond of drink. **Eisen**, the dwarf warrior, says little but looks out for everyone. And **Frieren**, the elf mage, goes through the journey with a kind of detachment.

The story opens with the Era Meteor Shower, a phenomenon that returns every fifty years. The party watches it together at the end of their quest, and Frieren promises to come back for the next one. Fifty years later, Himmel is an old man. He dies soon after, and it is at his funeral that Frieren bursts into tears, realizing that she never took the time to get to know him.

## The new journey

This regret drives Frieren to set out again. Heiter entrusts her with **Fern**, a war orphan he took in, who becomes her apprentice. Eisen, who says he is too old for adventure, recommends his student **Stark**, an axe-wielding warrior who constantly doubts himself but shows courage when it counts.

The trio heads north, partly retracing the route of Himmel's party. Their goal is Aureole, a land in Ende, in the far north, where the souls of the dead are said to rest. Frieren hopes to speak with Himmel one last time there. Along the way, they meet people the hero's party helped decades earlier, and everywhere they find the statues Himmel had built. He had explained why: he wanted Frieren, who would outlive him, not to feel alone.

## Magic

Magic in Frieren rests on **mana**, the energy every mage has in greater or lesser amounts and can sense in others. Flamme, Frieren's teacher, considered the founder of human magic, taught her a rule she has followed for more than a thousand years: always conceal your mana. Her opponents underestimate her, and that is one of her most effective weapons.

The story of **Zoltraak** shows how magic evolves. This spell, created by the demon Qual, once killed almost without fail, hence its nickname, “the magic that kills people”. During the roughly 80 years Qual remained sealed by Frieren, humans studied it, countered it with defensive magic, and made it the basis of ordinary offensive magic. When he wakes up, Qual discovers that his weapon has become a beginner's spell.

Frieren also has a less warlike passion: she collects useless spells. Removing rust from a statue, or making a field of flowers bloom, Flamme's favorite spell. These spells are often the reward she asks for her services. They also give her a recurring flaw: she opens every chest she comes across, even when they are obviously mimics.

## The demons

The demons in Frieren are not like the usual monsters. They talk, joke, mention their families. But for Frieren, this language is only a hunting tool: they imitate human speech to deceive their prey, without sharing human feelings. This rather disturbing idea comes up often in the series.

**Aura the Guillotine**, one of the Seven Sages of Destruction, is a good example. She uses the Scales of Obedience, which compare the mana of two opponents and make the weaker one submit to the stronger. Convinced she will win against Frieren's visible mana, she sets off the scales… and discovers how much mana Frieren was really hiding. The famous order Frieren gives her next, to kill herself, has become one of the best-known scenes of the anime.

## The First-Class Mage Exam

The end of the first season is devoted to the First-Class Mage Exam, organized by the Continental Magic Association of **Serie**, an elf even older and more powerful than Frieren, and Flamme's teacher. The exam introduces a gallery of very different human mages, from Denken, an old court mage who is not afraid to fight with his fists, to Übel, unpredictable and unsettling. Fern passes the exam. Frieren, on the other hand, is failed by Serie.

## German names

Most of the names come from German, and they say a lot. “Frieren” means to be cold or to freeze. “Himmel” means sky or heaven. “Eisen” is iron, “Stark” means strong, “Fern” far away, and “Heiter” cheerful. Spells and places often follow the same logic.

## The classic quiz traps

Flamme is Frieren's teacher, and Serie is Flamme's: the order is often reversed. Zoltraak was created by a demon, Qual, not by a human mage. Fern was raised by Heiter, not by Frieren, who only took her on as an apprentice afterwards. Finally, the journey of Himmel's party lasted ten years, and the meteor shower returns every fifty years: two numbers that are easy to mix up.

With these landmarks, the Frieren quiz becomes a good way to relive the series, from the first meteor shower to the exam.
