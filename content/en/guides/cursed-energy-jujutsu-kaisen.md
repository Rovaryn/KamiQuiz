Jujutsu Kaisen has a reputation for having one of the most technical power systems in recent manga. Its fights often look like games of chess, where every rule counts. Gege Akutami built a coherent but dense system. Here are the notions you need to follow the series and ace the quiz.

## Cursed energy

It all starts with human negative emotions: fear, anger, hatred. These emotions produce an energy called **cursed energy**. In most people, it builds up uncontrolled and ends up giving birth to **curses**, evil creatures invisible to ordinary people.

**Jujutsu sorcerers** are humans who can control their cursed energy and use it to fight curses. They are trained at two jujutsu schools, in Tokyo and Kyoto. Yuji Itadori, the hero, joins the Tokyo school after swallowing one of the fingers of Ryomen Sukuna, the King of Curses, and becoming his vessel.

## Grades

Sorcerers and curses are ranked by grade, from Grade 4, the lowest, to Grade 1, and then **Special Grade**, reserved for exceptional beings. Satoru Gojo is one of the few Special Grade sorcerers. Sukuna, the most feared of curses, is also in a class of his own.

## Innate techniques

Many sorcerers are born with an **innate technique**, a power of their own written into their body, which they fuel with their cursed energy. Some are passed down in the great jujutsu families, the Gojo, Zen'in and Kamo clans.

A few examples: Megumi Fushiguro uses the Ten Shadows Technique, which lets him summon creatures from his shadow. Nobara Kugisaki fights with a hammer, nails and a straw doll that transfers wounds. Aoi Todo instantly swaps places with whatever he wants by clapping his hands. Finally, Gojo combines Limitless, the technique of Infinity passed down in his clan, with the Six Eyes, an ocular ability that lets him perceive cursed energy with extreme precision.

Yuji, for his part, has no innate technique at the start of the series. He makes up for it with extraordinary physical strength and excellent hand-to-hand combat skills.

## Reversed energy

Normally, cursed energy destroys. But some sorcerers know how to reverse it, combining two negative energies to get a positive one. This **Reverse Cursed Technique** lets them heal their own wounds, or even other people's, a very rare ability. Shoko Ieiri, the doctor at the Tokyo school, is one of the only people who can heal others.

## Black Flash

A **Black Flash** happens when cursed energy strikes within a tiny interval after a blow lands. The power of the blow is then greatly amplified, and the energy turns black. No one can trigger it at will, but a sorcerer who pulls one off often finds themselves in a state of focus that helps them chain more. Yuji makes it one of his favorite weapons.

## Domain Expansions

**Domain Expansion** is the peak of mastery. The sorcerer creates an enclosed space, built from their cursed energy, inside which their technique becomes impossible to dodge. An attack launched inside it automatically hits its target.

The most famous are Gojo's, Unlimited Void, which floods its victims with information until they are paralyzed, and Sukuna's, Malevolent Shrine, which needs no walls to close and slices everything within a wide radius. To defend themselves, a sorcerer can deploy their own domain, or use simpler techniques, like **Simple Domain**, which cancels the automatic effect of an enemy domain within a small area.

## Binding vows

As in Hunter × Hunter, voluntary rules strengthen powers. A sorcerer can make a **binding vow**: impose a restriction on themselves or reveal their technique to their opponent to increase its effectiveness. Vows can also bind two people, and breaking them has serious consequences. The whole series plays on these agreements, which are often traps.

## The classic quiz traps

Yuji has no innate technique at the start: his strength comes from his body and, later, from Sukuna. The Six Eyes and Infinity are two different things, even though Gojo has both. Reverse Cursed Technique is used to heal, while Black Flash is used to hit harder. Finally, Simple Domain does not create a real domain: it only protects against the opponent's.

With these notions, the Jujutsu Kaisen quiz reads like the series itself: a matter of rules as much as strength.
