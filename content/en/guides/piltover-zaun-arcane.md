Arcane tells a story about sisters, but also a story about cities. Everything that happens to Vi and Jinx stems from a very precise setting: a gleaming city sitting on top of another one it would rather not see. Understanding this world means understanding why the series, created by Christian Linke and Alex Yee for Riot Games and animated by the French studio Fortiche, made such an impression. This article covers the broad outline of the first season and the start of the second, without revealing the ending.

## Two cities, one place

**Piltover** presents itself as the City of Progress. Rich, clean and devoted to science and trade, it is governed by a council on which the city's great families sit. Its inventors are celebrated every year on Progress Day.

**Zaun** spreads out below, in the depths. It is the city's former poor districts, which became a city in their own right, smoky, polluted and left to gangs and chemists. Its inhabitants often work for Piltover without sharing in its wealth. The gap between the city above and the undercity is not just an image: it is geographical, social and political, and it feeds every conflict in the series.

## Vander, Silco and the dream of a nation

Before the story begins, an uprising of the undercity's people against Piltover ended in bloodshed. Vi and Powder lost their parents in it. **Vander**, who led the revolt, took them in and then chose to keep the peace with the city above, even if it meant keeping order in the undercity himself.

**Silco**, his former friend, never accepted that choice. Vander tried to drown him, which left him with a scar around his eye and an endless grudge. Silco dreams of an independent Zaun, a free nation that would no longer bow to Piltover. To get there, he is ready to do anything, including poisoning his own people.

## Shimmer

Silco's weapon is **Shimmer**, a purple substance developed by the chemist **Singed**. It multiplies the strength of those who take it, to the point of turning some into real monsters, but slowly destroys them. Shimmer makes Silco rich, supplies him with superhuman henchmen and gives him leverage over those who depend on it. It is the symbol of an undercity that only survives by hurting itself.

## Hextech

Meanwhile, Piltover is going through its own revolution. **Jayce Talis**, a young inventor from the city above, and **Viktor**, his partner from Zaun, develop **hextech**, a technology that channels magic through gems. Their hextech gates, unveiled on Progress Day, allow almost instant transport and make the city's fortune.

**Heimerdinger**, a very long-lived yordle and member of the council, warns against progress that is too fast: poorly controlled magic has caused disasters before. His warnings are unwelcome, and the council ends up removing him. For Viktor, hextech is also personal. Ill since childhood, he hopes it can heal his people, and his work on the Hexcore, a form of hextech that can evolve, leads him down a dangerous path.

## The council and the great families

Piltover's council brings together the representatives of the great families. **Cassandra Kiramman**, Caitlyn's mother, sits on it. **Mel Medarda**, a brilliant and ambitious councilor, comes from a powerful family of Noxus, the neighboring military empire. Her mother, **Ambessa**, a formidable warlord, plays an important role in the second season. Through them, the series shows that Piltover is not just a city of scholars: it is also a city of merchants and political calculation, and it attracts the ambitions of foreign powers.

## Vi and Jinx, caught in the middle

The two sisters embody the divide between the cities. One night, trying to help, Powder sets off an explosion that kills their friends Mylo and Claggor, and Vander disappears in the same battle. Vi, arrested, spends years in Stillwater prison. Powder is taken in by Silco, who raises her as his daughter, and becomes **Jinx**, the one who brings bad luck.

When Vi gets out of prison, it is thanks to **Caitlyn**, a young Piltover enforcer who needs her to investigate in the undercity. Their partnership links the two worlds. Jinx, for her part, is torn between the sister she lost and the surrogate father who saved her. At the end of the first season, she fires a rocket at Piltover's council chamber, and this act tips the two cities into war.

## The Firelights and Ekko

Not all of Zaun belongs to Silco. **Ekko**, Powder's childhood friend, leads the **Firelights**, a group of young rebels sheltering around a giant tree. They fight Silco and his Shimmer, and dream of a freer undercity. The confrontation between Ekko and Jinx, on a bridge, is one of the most powerful moments of the first season, because it pits two children who grew up together against each other.

## The classic quiz traps

Shimmer is a drug from Zaun, while hextech is a technology from Piltover: the two are often confused. Viktor comes from Zaun, not from the city above like Jayce. Mel Medarda is from Noxus, and Ambessa is her mother, not her sister. Finally, Jinx was called Powder as a child, and she was raised by Vander, then Silco, not by her parents.

With these landmarks, the Arcane quiz becomes a good way to relive the series, from the undercity all the way to Piltover's council.
