“Who is the strongest?” is the question every Naruto fan asks sooner or later. Masashi Kishimoto never published an official ranking, but the fights in the series reveal a fairly clear hierarchy at the top. Here are the characters who come up in every discussion, and what earns them their place. This article discusses the ending of Naruto Shippuden.

## Above everyone: Kaguya Ōtsutsuki

Kaguya is where it all began. By eating the fruit of the God Tree, she gained chakra and became the first person to master it. She combines the Rinne Sharingan, the ability to create and cross entire dimensions, and absolute control over the beings linked to the God Tree. It takes Naruto and Sasuke together, at the peak of their abilities, to seal her in the final arc.

## The Sage of Six Paths

Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki, Kaguya's son, founded ninshu, the ancestor of ninjutsu. With his brother Hamura, he sealed their mother, then split the chakra of the Ten-Tails into nine Tailed Beasts. His two sons, Indra and Asura, are the source of the rivalry between the Uchiha and the Senju, which is reincarnated all the way down to Sasuke and Naruto.

## Naruto and Sasuke at the end of the series

At the end of the Fourth Great Ninja War, Naruto and Sasuke each receive part of the Sage of Six Paths' power. Naruto has the chakra of all nine Tailed Beasts and Six Paths Sage Mode. Sasuke gains the Rinnegan in his left eye and a perfect Susanoo. Their final battle, at the Valley of the End, destroys part of the landscape, and neither of them truly wins: the best proof that they are in the same league.

## Madara Uchiha

Madara is the great antagonist of the war. First brought back by Kabuto's Reanimation Jutsu (Edo Tensei), he later regains a real body, then becomes the jinchuriki of the Ten-Tails. By then he has the Rinnegan, the Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan and Hashirama's cells. Five Kage together cannot beat him while he is still only a reanimated soul, which says a lot about his power.

## Hashirama Senju

The First Hokage is the only ninja Madara ever saw as his equal. Hashirama founded the Hidden Leaf Village and mastered Wood Release, which lets him, among other things, control the Tailed Beasts. His regeneration and his chakra reserves make him one of the most formidable fighters in the history of the ninja world, and his role in founding the village gives him a unique importance.

## Obito Uchiha

After becoming the jinchuriki of the Ten-Tails during the war, Obito reaches a level of power comparable to that of the Sage of Six Paths, so much that the Allied Shinobi Forces struggle to even touch him. Before that, his mastery of Kamui, which lets him make his body intangible and teleport, already made him an almost unbeatable opponent.

## The debated mentions

Several characters, less powerful in absolute terms, are often cited for their feats. **Itachi Uchiha** has the Mangekyō Sharingan, Tsukuyomi and a Susanoo armed with the Totsuka Blade and the Yata Mirror, two almost unstoppable artifacts. **Minato Namikaze**, the Fourth Hokage, owes his nickname, the Yellow Flash of the Hidden Leaf, to the Flying Thunder God technique, which lets him teleport instantly. Finally, **Might Guy** opens the Eighth Gate against Madara and lands blows that impress him, at the cost of his own life, which Naruto saves at the last moment.

## Why the rankings differ

The debates come from the fact that power in Naruto depends a lot on circumstances. A technique like Tsukuyomi or Kamui can decide a fight against an opponent who is much stronger on paper. A character's abilities also change over the series: the Kakashi of the early chapters has nothing in common with the one who briefly gains the Mangekyō in both eyes during the war.

For the Naruto quiz, it is better to remember facts than opinions: who sealed Kaguya, who founded the Hidden Leaf, which Hokage had which nickname, and which eyes each Uchiha has. Those are the details the hard questions check.
