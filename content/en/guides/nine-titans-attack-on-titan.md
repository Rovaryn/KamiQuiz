Attack on Titan begins as a story about humans besieged by mindless giants. But over the seasons, the series reveals that nine very special Titans, all from a single source, have shaped the history of its world. This article goes through each of them. It contains spoilers for the whole series.

## A single origin

According to the legend of Eldia, a young girl named Ymir Fritz gained the power of the Titans around two thousand years ago when she came into contact with a mysterious source at the foot of a tree. When she died, her power was split into nine parts among her descendants. These are the Nine Titans.

A human of Eldian descent can be turned into a mindless Pure Titan by an injection of Titan spinal fluid. If a Pure Titan devours someone who holds one of the Nine Titans, it becomes human again and inherits the power. This is the principle of inheritance on which the whole plot rests.

## The Curse of Ymir

Inheriting one of the Nine Titans comes at a price. A holder lives only thirteen years after gaining the power, the length of time Ymir is said to have lived after receiving hers. This rule explains the urgency that drives several characters, and the cruel logic of Marley, which passes its Titans from one Eldian child warrior to the next.

Holders also sometimes share memories of their predecessors, and even of their successors in the case of the Attack Titan. These memories play a decisive role in the last part of the series.

## The Nine Titans

**The Founding Titan.** It can control the other Titans and alter the memories of Eldians. Its full power can only be used by a member of the royal family, or by a holder in contact with someone of that bloodline. Long held by the Reiss family, it passes to Grisha Yeager and then to his son Eren.

**The Attack Titan.** It stands out for its fierce will to keep moving forward, and its special power lets its holder see the memories of future holders. Passed from Eren Kruger to Grisha Yeager, it then goes to Eren.

**The Colossal Titan.** Around sixty meters tall, it gives off enormous heat and steam. It is the one that breaches Wall Maria at the start of the series. Bertholdt Hoover holds it before Armin Arlert inherits it.

**The Armored Titan.** Covered in hardened plates, it serves as both shield and battering ram. Its holder, Reiner Braun, is one of the most tormented characters in the series.

**The Female Titan.** Versatile, it can harden parts of its body and draw Pure Titans with a scream. Annie Leonhart holds it.

**The Beast Titan.** Covered in fur, it throws projectiles with fearsome accuracy. Zeke Yeager, Eren's half-brother and a member of the royal bloodline, holds it.

**The Jaw Titan.** Small, fast and armed with jaws that can crush almost anything. It passes from Marcel Galliard to Ymir, then to Porco Galliard, and finally to Falco Grice.

**The Cart Titan.** A four-legged Titan, it can stay transformed for a very long time and carry equipment. Pieck Finger holds it.

**The War Hammer Titan.** It makes hardened weapons and structures rise from the ground, while its holder stays protected inside a crystal at a distance. Long kept by the Tybur family, it is stolen by Eren during the attack on Liberio.

## Hardening and crystallization

Several Titans can harden their skin, but the Armored Titan and the War Hammer Titan have made it their specialty. A holder can also shut themselves inside an almost indestructible crystal, as Annie does after her capture. These powers are essential to understanding how the Walls were built: they are themselves made of hardened Colossal Titans.

## The classic quiz traps

Several details come up often in the Attack on Titan quiz. Grisha Yeager held two Titans at once, the Attack Titan and the Founding Titan, before passing both on to Eren. The Jaw Titan passes between four holders over the course of the series, which makes the question of who holds it easy to get wrong. Ymir, Christa's comrade, has nothing to do with Ymir Fritz, even though they share the same name. Finally, the curse counts thirteen years from the moment the power is inherited, not from birth.

With these landmarks in mind, the category on the Titans becomes much more approachable.
